The plain form is necessary to make quotes.

Using this approach you can say something like:
- Ian said he is tired.
- He said he likes Kimchi.
- Did you say pizza?

## The basics

There are different quoting forms, depending on whether the original quote was a question, suggestion, command or regular statement.

## Quoting a statement

`Plain form + 고 하다` 

1. Conjugate the plain form of any verb.
2. Attach `고`.
3. Add the verb `말(을) 하다` (to speak) and conjugate.

The `하다` at the end is a shortened `말하다`. It's also possible to omit the `하다` completely.

Usually the longer form of `-다고요` is used when you're repeating, confirming, or emphasizing something that was just said. Use the short `-다고 했어요` form when you're simply reporting what someone said.

**Examples**:
- I said I like movies: 저는 영화를 좋아한다고 했어요. / 저는 영화를 좋아한다고요.
- Did you say you don't have time?: 시간이 없다고 했어요? / 시간이 없다고요?
- I said it's too expensive: 너무 바싸다고 했어요. / 너무 비싸다고요
- I said I'm also a foreigner: 저도 외국인이라고 했어요.

**Exceptions**:
- `이다` and `아니다` are exceptions.
    - `이다` becomes `이라고` after a consonant; or `라고` after a vowel.
    - `아니다` becomes `아니라고`.

## Quoting a question

Use this form when quoting what someone asked.

`Verb stem + 냐고 하다`

1. Get the verb stem.
2. Attach `냐고`.
3. Add the verb `하다` and conjugate like before. Or add the verb `물어보다` (to ask).

**Examples**:
- Did you ask me if I speak Korean well?: 제가 한국어를 잘 하냐고 물어봤어요?
- My friend asked where I was going: 친구가 어디에 가냐고 물어봤어요.

**Special conjugation cases**:
- If the verb stem ends with a `ㄹ`, remove `ㄹ` and attach `냐고`.
    - For example: `살다` -> `사냐고`.
- If the verb stem ends with a `ㅂ`, remove `ㅂ` and attach `우` then `냐고`.
    - For example: `맵다` -> `매우냐고`.
- `이다` will become `이냐고` after a consonant; or after a vowel `냐고`.

## Quoting a command

Use this form when quoting a command.

`Verb stem + (으)라고 하다`

1. Get the verb stem.
2. Attach `으라고` if it ends in a consonant; or `라고` if it ends in a vowel.
3. Add the verb `하다` and conjugate like before.

**Examples**:
- I said turn off the lights: 불을 끄라고 했어요.
- My mom told me to eat: 엄마가 밥을 먹으라고 했어요.

**Special conjugation cases**:
- If the verb stem ends in a `ㄹ`, attach `라고`.
    - For example: `살다` (to live) -> `살라고`
- If the verb stem ends in a `ㅂ`, remove `ㅂ`, attach `우`, then attach `라고`.
    - For example: `눕다` (to lie down) → `누우라고`

## Quoting a suggestion

Use this form when quoting a suggestion (Let's).

`Verb stem + 자고 하다`

1. Get the verb stem.
2. Attach `자고`.
3. Add the verb `하다` and conjugate like before.

**Examples**:
- Did you say let's go eat Kimbap?: 김밥을 먹으러 가자고요?
- My friend suggested going home: 친구가 집에 가자고 했어요.
