How do you say "should" or "should not" in Korean? Several [ways to make suggestions were covered in an earlier chapter](/chapter/making-suggestions). This chapter builds on that by introducing the `(으)면` form with `좋다` to give a gentle recommendation ("it would be good to…" or "you should…"), and with `안 되다` to express a warning or prohibition ("you shouldn't…" or "you must not…").

## Saying "should" with (으)면 좋다

To say `should` combine the `(으)면` form with `좋다` (To be good). It literally means `It's good if...`.

<Sentence
    ko="지금 떠나**면 좋아요**."
    en="You should depart now."
/>

## Saying "shouldn't" with (으)면 안 되다

To say `shouldn't` combine the `(으)면` form with `안 되다` (To not be okay). It literally means `It's not okay if...`.

<Sentence
    ko="지금 떠나**면 안 돼요**."
    en="You shouldn't depart now."
/>
