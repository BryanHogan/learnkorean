title: "Should and shouldn't"
description: How to say `should` or `should not` in Korean?
---

How to say `should` or `should not` in Korean? Multiple [ways to make suggestion were covered by a previous chapter](/chapter/making-suggestions). This chapter builds on that by introducing the `(으)면` form with `좋다` to give a gentle recommendation ("it would be good to…" or "you should…"), and with `안 되다` to express a warning or prohibition ("you shouldn't…" or "you must not…").

## Saying `should` using the (으)면 form with 좋다

To say `should` combine the `(으)면` form with `좋다` (To be good). It literally means "`It's good if..."`.

<Sentence
    ko="지금 떠나**면 좋아요**."
    en="You should depart now."
/>

## Saying `shouldn't` using the (으)면 form with 안 되다

To say `shouldn't` combine the `(으)면` form with `안 되다` (To not be okay). It literally means "`It's not okay if..."`.

<Sentence
    ko="지금 떠나면 안 돼요."
    en="You shouldn't depart now."
/>
