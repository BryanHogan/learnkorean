 How to say a noun is becoming something in Korean using the verb `되다`.

## Becoming something using 되다

Content

**Example**:
- What do you want to become? → `뭐가 되고 싶어요?`
- I want to become a nicer person → `저는 더 친전한 사람이 되고 싶어요.`
- I want to become a Korean teacher → `저는 한국어 선생님이 되고 싶어요.`

## To be okay (permission) using 되다

`되다` can also be used to give permission as in "to be okay".

**Examples**:
- Is it really okay to go? Can I really go? → `정말 가도 돼요?`
- It's not okay → `안 돼요.`

## For something to work using 되다

`되다` can also be used say something works, e.g. to say a machine works properly.

**Examples**:
- The machine doesn't work now. → `이 기계가 지금 안 돼요.`
- The printer suddenly doesn't work. → `프린터가 갑자기 안 돼요.`
- My cell phone works well. → `제 핸드폰이 잘 돼요.`

## Asking to tell about something using 되다

`되다` can also be used wih the adverb `어떻게` (how) to ask someone to tell you about something. This is a more polite way to ask a person's name, age or information about themselves.

**Examples**:
- Tell me about your family. → `가족이 어떻게 되세요?`
- Tell me about your hobbies. / What are your hobbies? → `취미가 어떻게 돼요?`
- Tell me about your name. / What is your name? → `이름이 어떻게 돼요?`
- Tell me about your age. / How old are you? → `나이가 어떻게 돼요?`
- Tell me about your age. / How old are you? (More polite than 나이) → `연세가 어떻게 되세요?`
