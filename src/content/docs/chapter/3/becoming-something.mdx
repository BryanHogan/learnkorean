---
title: "Becoming Something and Other Uses of 되다"
description: How to say someone or something is becoming something in Korean using the verb 되다 and other uses of 되다.
---


`되다` can be used for several things, including becoming something, asking for or giving permission, describing whether something works, and asking for personal information.

## Becoming something using 되다

`되다` can be used to say that you are becoming something.

**Examples**:

<Sentence
  ko="뭐가 **되고** 싶어요?"
  en="What do you want to become?"
/>

<Sentence
  ko="저는 더 친절한 사람이 **되고** 싶어요."
  en="I want to become a nicer person"
/>

<Sentence
  ko="저는 한국어 선생님이 **되고** 싶어요."
  en="I want to become a Korean teacher"
/>

## To be okay (permission) using 되다

`되다` can also be used to give permission as in "to be okay".

**Examples**:

<Sentence
  ko="정말 가도 **돼요**?"
  en="Is it really okay to go? Can I really go?"
/>

<Sentence
  ko="안 **돼요**."
  en="It's not okay"
/>

## For something to work using 되다

`되다` can also be used say something works, e.g. to say a machine works properly.

**Examples**:

<Sentence
  ko="이 기계가 지금 안 **돼요**."
  en="The machine doesn't work now."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="프린터가 갑자기 안 **돼요**."
  en="The printer suddenly doesn't work."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="제 핸드폰이 잘 **돼요**."
  en="My cell phone works well."
/>

## Asking to tell about something using 되다

`되다` can also be used with the adverb `어떻게` (how) to ask someone to tell you about something. This is a more polite way to ask a person's name, age or information about themselves.

**Examples**:

<Sentence
  ko="가족이 어떻게 **되세요**?"
  en="Tell me about your family."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="취미가 어떻게 **돼요**?"
  en="Tell me about your hobbies. / What are your hobbies?"
/>

<Sentence
  ko="이름이 어떻게 **돼요**?"
  en="Tell me about your name. / What is your name?"
/>

<Sentence
  ko="나이가 어떻게 **돼요**?"
  en="Tell me about your age. / How old are you?"
/>

<Sentence
  ko="연세가 어떻게 **되세요**?"
  en="Tell me about your age. / How old are you? (More polite than 나이)"
/>
