This chapter will cover how to say before and after in Korean.

## Saying `before`

To say something happens before something use the word `전`:

`Noun + 전에`

For example:

<Sentence
    ko="시험 **전에** 공부를 했어요."
    en="I studied before the test."
/>

The noun used here can also be an [action verb changed into a noun using the 기 form](/chapter/describing-verbs/#nominalization-using-기). For example: 

<Sentence
    ko="시험을 보기 **전에** 공부를 했어요."
    en="I studied before taking the test."
/>

**Example sentences using `전`**:

<Sentence
    ko="그 **전에** 만날 수 있을까요?"
    en="Could we (be able to) meet before then?"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="세 시 **전에** 오세요."
    en="Come before 3 o'clock."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="점심을 먹기 **전에** 손을 씻고 싶어요."
    en="I want to wash my hands before eating lunch."
/>

## Ways to say `after`

There are multiple ways to say after in Korean, so let's cover the following ways in this chapter: `후`, `-고`, `뒤` and `다음`.

### Saying `after` using `후`

To say something happens after something you can use the word `후`. Apply `후` after a noun, followed by the particle `에`:

`Noun + 후에`

<Sentence
    ko="수업 **후에** 공부를 할 거예요."
    en="I will study after class."
/>

For **action verbs** this rule works slightly differently:

`Action Verb Stem + (은/ㄴ) 후에`

1. Get the stem of an action verb.
2. Depending on the ending:
    - If it ends in a consonant: Attach `은 후`.
    - If it ends in a vowel: Attach `ㄴ 후`.
    - However, if it ends in a `ㄹ`: Remove the `ㄹ`, then attach `ㄴ 후에`.
3. Attach the particle `에`.


**Example sentences using `후`**:

<Sentence
    ko="수업을 **들은 후에** 공부를 할 거예요."
    en="I will study after I have class. / I will study after attending class."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="오늘 다섯 시 **후에** 시간이 있을 거예요."
    en="Today I will have time after 5 o'clock."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="영화를 **본 후에** 친구랑 놀러 갔어요."
    en="I went to hang out with a friend after seeing the movie."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="도착**한 후에** 전화 주세요."
    en="Give me a call after you arrive."
/>

### Saying after using `고`

The `고` form can be used to mean `and` as well as `and then`, connecting a sequence of actions. Learn more about the [`고` form in this previous chapter](/).

**Example sentences using `고`**:

<Sentence
    ko="저는 밥을 **먹고** 운동을 할 거예요."
    en="I will eat a meal and then exercise."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="저는 공부를 **하고** 시험을 봤어요."
    en="I studied and then took the test."
/>


### Saying after using `뒤` or `다음`

Both `뒤` and `다음` can be used the same way as `후`, especially with action verbs.

`뒤` meaning behind becomes `뒤에`.

<Sentence
    ko="아침을 먹**은 뒤에** 친구를 만나러 갔어요."
    en="After eating breakfast I went to meet a friend."
/>

`다음` meaning `(the) next` becomes `다음에`.

<Sentence
    ko="아침을 먹**은 다음에** 친구를 만나러 갔어요."
    en="After eating breakfast I went to meet a friend."
/>
