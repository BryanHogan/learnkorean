---
title: "Or in Korean"
description: How can you say "or" in Korean?
---

This chapter will cover two ways to say `or` in Korean.

`Or` is often used when deciding between more than one choice. But it can also be used when there is no choice.

## Saying `or` using `아니면`

The `아니면` form is used whenever there is **a choice to be made**. It can be used between two nouns or at the beginning of a sentence. It literally means "if it is not" and comes from `아니다` ("to not be").

<Sentence
    ko="바닷가 **아니면** 산도 괜찮아요."
    en="The beach or the mountains are okay."
/>

Usually it's used at the beginning of a sentence.

<Sentence
    ko="바닷가에 갈까요? **아니면** 산에 갈까요?"
    en="Shall we go to the beach, or the mountains?"
/>

Since `아니면` is used for making a choice it's most often used for asking a question, such as asking someone to make a choice.

Example sentences:

<Sentence
    ko="치즈 피자를 먹을 거예요? **아니면** 소시지 피자를 먹을 거예요?"
    en="Are you going to eat a cheese pizza, or a sausage pizza?"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="젓가락을 쓰고 싶어요? **아니면** 포크를 쓰고 싶어요?"
    en="Do you want to use chopsticks, or a fork?"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="조금 더 기다릴까요? **아니면** 그냥 갈까요?"
    en="Shall we wait a little more, or shall we just go?"
/>

## Saying `or` using `(이)나`

`(이)나` is another common way to say `or`. `(이)나` is used when listing more than one option. `(이)나` is **not** used when making a choice. So using it means that the options do not matter, any of them is fine, or none of them is fine as well. A literal translation for it would be something like "or something".

`(이)나` is used between two nouns. Attach `이나` after a noun that ends in a consonant, or attach `나` after a noun that ends in a vowel.

Example sentences:

<Sentence
    ko="영화**나** 드라마를 볼 거예요."
    en="I will watch a movie or drama. (*This means that you might watch a movie or drama, or something. In fact, you might not watch anything and that's okay too*.)"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="옷**이나** 모자를 살 거예요."
    en="I'll buy clothes or a hat."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="서울**이나** 부산에 여행 가고 싶어요."
    en="I want to go on a trip to Seoul or Busan."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="따뜻한 커피**나** 우유를 드릴까요?"
    en="Shall I give you some warm coffee or milk?"
/>
