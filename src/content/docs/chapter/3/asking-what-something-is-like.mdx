## Outline

- Introduce 어떻다 as the interrogative counterpart to 이렇다, 그렇다, and 저렇다: “to be how” or “to be what like.”
- Explain the relationship between 어떻다 and its common forms 어떤, 어때요, and 어땠어요.
- Use 어떤 before a noun to ask what kind of person or thing it is.
- Use 어때요 to ask about a present quality, condition, opinion, or suggestion.
- Use 어땠어요 to ask what a past event, experience, or condition was like.
- Distinguish 어떻다 and 어때요 from 어떻게, which asks how an action is performed.
- Briefly defer the detailed comparison of 어떤, 무슨, and 어느 to the later question-word chapter.
- Finish with a short dialogue and practice selecting 어떤, 어때요, or 어땠어요.
