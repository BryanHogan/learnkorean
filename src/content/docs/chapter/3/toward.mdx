---
title: "Toward using (으)로"
description: How to say toward something using the particle (으)로.
---


The particle `(으)로` means "*toward*" or "*in the direction of*". Usually it will translate naturally to "*to*".

It has similarities to the particle `에` which is used to mark a location that something is going to or the location at which something exists.

`(으)로` can **not** be used to mark the location at which something exists, making it different from `에`.

Usually when saying "toward: or "to" a location the particle `(으)로` will sound more natural than `에`.

## How to use `(으)로`

- Attach `으로` to a noun ending with a consonant.
- Attach `로` to a noun ending with a vowel, or ending with the letter `ㄹ`.

## Examples of using `(으)로`

<Sentence
  ko="우리 집**으로** 오세요."
  en="Come toward our house."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="저쪽**으로** 가세요."
  en="Go that way."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="이쪽**으로** 천천히 오세요."
  en="Come this way slowly."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="저는 지금 집**으로** 가고 있어요."
  en="I am now going toward home. / I am going home."
/>

## More uses of `(으)로`

`(으)로` can also be used for saying something gets used, which will be covered in the next chapter. (`(으)로` has even more uses actually.)
