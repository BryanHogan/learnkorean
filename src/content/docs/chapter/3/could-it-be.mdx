How to say `"Could it be?"` in Korean.

In a previous chapter we already covered [suggestions such as "shall we / I?"](/chapter/making-suggestions). This form will also be used in this chapter.

## Using `(을/ㄹ)까요` with descriptive verbs

The previous chapter covered only action verbs, but it can also be used with descriptive verbs.

For `ㅎ`-irregular descriptive verbs, remove `ㅎ`, then attach `ㄹ까요`: 그렇다 → 그럴까요? (Could that be so?).

Regular `ㅎ` stems keep `ㅎ` and take `을까요`: 좋다 → 좋을까요? (Would it be good?).

## Asking `could?` or `would?` using `(을/ㄹ)까요`

This form can also mean `could?` or `would?`, so asking about whether something is possible or not.

<Sentence
    ko="내일 비가 **올까요**?"
    en="Could it rain tomorrow?"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="이안 씨는 지금 뭘 만들고 **있을까요**?"
    en="What could Ian be making now?"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="도와줄 수 **있을까요**?"
    en="Would you be able to help?"
/>
