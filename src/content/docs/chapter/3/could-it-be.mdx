How to say `"Could it be?"` in Korean.

In a previous chapter we already covered suggestions such as "shall we / I?". For example:

<Sentence
    ko="제가 도와**줄까요**?"
    en="Shall I help?"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="이제 저녁을 먹**을까요**?"
    en="Shall we eat dinner now?"
/>

The previous chapter covered only action verbs, but it can also be used with descriptive verbs.

This form can also mean `could?` or `would?`, so asking about whether something is possible or not.
