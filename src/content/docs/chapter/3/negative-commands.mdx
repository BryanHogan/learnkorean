---
title: "Don't do it: Negative commands"
description: How to tell someone in Korean to not do something, how to build negative commands.
---

You can tell someone not to do something with a negative command.

## Building negative commands

`Action Verb Stem + 지 말다`

1. Get the action verb stem.
2. Attach `지 말다`, and conjugate it.

<Sentence
    ko="하**지 마세요**."
    en="Don't do it."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="가**지 마세요**."
    en="Don't go."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="저를 미워하**지 마세요**."
    en="Don't hate me."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="저를 보**지 말아** 주세요."
    en="Please don't look at me."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="포기하**지 마**."
    en="Don't give up. (Casual)"
/>

Note: *`지 마` is only acceptable in casual speech (반말).*

<Sentence
    ko="그건 먹**지 마요**."
    en="Don't eat that. (Informal polite)"
/>

Note: *`지 마요` is only acceptable in informal polite speech (존댓말).*

This `지 말다` form also works with other grammar forms:

<Sentence
    ko="가**지 말까요**?"
    en="Shall I not go?"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="계란을 사**지 말자**."
    en="Let's not buy eggs."
/>

## Related concepts: `아니고` and `말고`

You can use `아니고` and `말고` when you want to say "`not (noun)`". These are usually used between two nouns for this purpose.

### Using `아니고`

`아니고` comes from the descriptive verb `아니다` (to not be), and literally means "`it's not... and...`".

`Noun이/가 아니고`:

<Sentence
    ko="저는 한국인이 **아니고** 독일인이에요."
    en="I'm German, not Korean."
/>

### Using `말고`

`말고` comes from the action verb `말다` (to stop doing, to not do) and literally means "`don't do... but..."`.

`Noun 말고`:

<Sentence
    ko="콜라 **말고** 물을 주세요."
    en="Give me water, not cola."
/>
