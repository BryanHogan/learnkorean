---
title: "Using something with (으)로"
description: Learn how to use (으)로 to express a tool, material, method, or means of transportation.
---


The particle `(으)로` can also be used to show what sort of method, material, machine, tool, channels or means were used with which something happens. For example this can translate to "using", "by" or "with" among some other options.

Two examples:

<Sentence
  ko="학교에 기차**로** 갔어요."
  en="I went to school by train."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="편지를 연필**로** 썼어요."
  en="I wrote a letter with a pencil."
/>

## How to attach `(으)로`

The attachment rule is the same as in the previous chapter:

- Attach `으로` to a noun ending with a consonant.
- Attach `로` to a noun ending with a vowel, or ending with the letter `ㄹ`.

##  Further examples

<Sentence
  ko="비행기 세 대를 종이**로** 만들었어요."
  en="I made three airplanes out of paper."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="영어**로** 말해 주세요."
  en="Please speak in English."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="손**으로** 먹었어요."
  en="I ate with my hands."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="이따가 이메일**로** 보내 주세요."
  en="Please send it by email a little bit later."
/>
