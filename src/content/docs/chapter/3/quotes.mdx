title: "Making quotes"
description: How to quote in Korean and say you said something.
---


The plain form is necessary to make quotes.

Using this approach you can say something like:
- Ian said he is tired.
- He said he likes Kimchi.
- Did you say pizza?

## The basics

There are different quoting forms, depending on whether the original quote was a question, suggestion, command or regular statement.

### Quoting a statement

`Plain form + 고 하다`

1. Conjugate the plain form of any verb.
2. Attach `고`.
3. Add the verb `말(을) 하다` (to speak) and conjugate.

You can use just `하다` instead of `말하다`. It's also possible to omit the `하다` completely.

Use `-다고 했어요` to report what someone said. Use `-다고요` to repeat or emphasize your own words, or with a question mark to confirm what someone else said.

**Examples**:

<Sentence
  ko="저는 영화를 좋아한**다고** 했어요. / 저는 영화를 좋아한**다고**요."
  en="I said I like movies"
/>

<Sentence
  ko="시간이 없**다고** 했어요? / 시간이 없**다고**요?"
  en="Did you say you don't have time?"
/>

<Sentence
  ko="너무 비싸**다고** 했어요. / 너무 비싸**다고**요."
  en="I said it's too expensive"
/>

<Sentence
  ko="저도 외국인**이라고** 했어요."
  en="I said I'm also a foreigner"
/>

**Exceptions**:
- `이다` and `아니다` are exceptions.
    - `이다` becomes `이라고` after a consonant; or `라고` after a vowel.
    - `아니다` becomes `아니라고`.

### Quoting a question

Use this form when quoting what someone asked.

`Verb stem + 냐고 하다`

1. Get the verb stem.
2. Attach `냐고`.
3. Add the verb `하다` and conjugate like before. Or add the verb `물어보다` (to ask).

**Examples**:

<Sentence
  ko="제가 한국어를 잘하**냐고** 물어봤어요?"
  en="Did you ask me if I speak Korean well?"
/>

<Sentence
  ko="친구가 어디에 가**냐고** 물어봤어요."
  en="My friend asked where I was going"
/>

**Special conjugation cases**:
- If the verb stem ends with a `ㄹ`, remove `ㄹ` and attach `냐고`.
    - For example: `살다` -> `사냐고`.

- If an irregular verb stem ends with a `ㅂ`, remove `ㅂ` and attach `우` then `냐고`.
    - For example: `맵다` -> `매우냐고`.

- `이다` will become `이냐고` after a consonant; or after a vowel `냐고`.

### Quoting a command

Use this form when quoting a command.

`Verb stem + (으)라고 하다`

1. Get the verb stem.
2. Attach `으라고` if it ends in a consonant; or `라고` if it ends in a vowel.
3. Add the verb `하다` and conjugate like before.

**Examples**:

<Sentence
  ko="불을 끄**라고** 했어요."
  en="I said turn off the lights"
/>

<Sentence
  ko="엄마가 밥을 먹**으라고** 했어요."
  en="My mom told me to eat"
/>

**Special conjugation cases**:
- If the verb stem ends in a `ㄹ`, attach `라고`.
    - For example: `살다` (to live) -> `살라고`.

- If an irregular verb stem ends in a `ㅂ`, remove `ㅂ`, attach `우`, then attach `라고`.
    - For example: `눕다` (to lie down) -> `누우라고`.

    - With a regular verb, keep the `ㅂ`: `입다` (to wear) -> `입으라고`.


The verb `주다` (to give) has a special form when used with the command quoting form:
- It becomes `달라고` when the speaker is also the person who is receiving something.
- It becomes `주라고` when the speaker is not the person who is receiving something.
- This also applies when using 주다 after a conjugated action verb.
- For example:

    <Sentence
      ko="저는 민지 씨에게 **주라고** 했어요."
      en="I said give it to Minji"
    />

    <Sentence
      ko="저는 **달라고** 했어요."
      en="I said give it to me"
    />

    <Sentence
      ko="저는 해 **달라고** 했어요."
      en="I said do it for me"
    />

### Quoting a suggestion

Use this form when quoting a suggestion (Let's).

`Verb stem + 자고 하다`

1. Get the verb stem.
2. Attach `자고`.
3. Add the verb `하다` and conjugate like before.

**Examples**:

<Sentence
  ko="김밥을 먹으러 가**자고**요?"
  en="Did you say let's go eat Kimbap?"
/>

<Sentence
  ko="친구가 집에 가**자고** 했어요."
  en="My friend suggested going home"
/>

## Topic and subject markers when quoting

Usually the person who is saying the quote is marked by the [Topic Marker](/chapter/topic-marker) (은/는).

The subject of the quote, usually who or what the quote is talking about, uses the [Subject Marker](/chapter/subject-marker) (이/가).

This is not a strong rule, but usually this is how they will be used.

**Example**:

<Sentence
  ko="저**는** 김치**가** 맛있다고 했어요."
  en="I said Kimchi is delicious"
/>
