---
title: "Verb ending 죠 for wanting agreement"
description: How to express agreement using the 죠 ending.
---

To express that you want someone to agree with something, e.g. when saying something like "It's cold, isn't it?" you can use `죠` at the end of a sentence. `죠` is a contraction of `지요`. 

`Verb stem + 죠`

1. Get the verb stem.
2. Attach 죠. In casual speech, use `지` instead of `죠`.

<Sentence
    ko="한국말이 어렵**죠**?"
    en="Korean is difficult, right?"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="가기 싫**죠**?"
    en="You don't want to go, do you?"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="화장을 했**죠**?"
    en="You did your makeup, right?"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="날씨가 춥**지**?"
    en="It's cold, isn't it?"
/>
