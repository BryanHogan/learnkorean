title: Saying "It Is Like This" with 이렇다, 그렇다, and 저렇다
description: Learn how 이렇다, 그렇다, and 저렇다 describe something as being this way, that way, or that way over there.
unit: 3
---

## Outline

- Introduce 이렇다, 그렇다, and 저렇다 as descriptive verbs meaning “to be this way,” “to be that way,” and “to be that way over there.”
- Explain how 이-, 그-, and 저- point to something near the speaker, near the listener or already mentioned, and away from both.
- Form and use the common polite forms 이래요, 그래요, and 저래요.
- Introduce the noun-modifying forms 이런, 그런, and 저런.
- Show the past-tense forms 이랬어요, 그랬어요, and 저랬어요 in context.
- Distinguish these forms from 이것/그것/저것 and 이렇게/그렇게/저렇게.
- Finish with a short dialogue and practice choosing the appropriate form.
