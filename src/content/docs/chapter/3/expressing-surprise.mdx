The verb ending `네요` is used to express surprise.

## How to use `네요` to express surprise

Just add `네요` to the verb stem:

`Verb stem + 네요`

1. Get the verb stem.
2. Attach 네, followed by 요. In [casual speech](chapter/casual-speech) the 요 is omitted.
    - If the verb stem ends in a `ㄹ`, remove the `ㄹ` and then add `네요`.

<Sentence
    ko="이 김치가 정말 **맵네요**."
    en="This kimchi is really spicy (and I'm surprised)."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="그 학교가 너무 **머네요**."
    en="That school is really far (and I'm surprised)."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="그 방법을 점 **느리네요**."
    en="That method is a little slow (and I'm surprised)."
/>
