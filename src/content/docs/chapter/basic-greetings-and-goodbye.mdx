How to say hello and goodbye.

## Saying hello

Saying "hello" actually it depends on who you are talking to. Korean has different politeness levels.

To close friends at the same age as you and younger: `안녕`

To anyone else: `안녕하세요`

## Saying goodbye

To close friends at the same age as you and younger: `안녕`

Yes, "goodbye" is the same word as saying "hello".

But to other people, when you need to be more formal, you have two options:

- You are leaving, they are staying: `안녕히 가세요` ("*Go in peace!*")
- You are staying, they are leaving: `안녕히 게세요` ("*Stay in peace!*")
