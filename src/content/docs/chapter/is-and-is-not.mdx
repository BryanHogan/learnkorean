## Introducing your name with 이다

To say I am Ian you can say: `저는 이안이에요`  
To say your name is Sarah: `저는 사라예요`

`저는` comes from `저` (meaning I or me) and 는 which will be covered later in this unit.

`이에요` and `예요` both come from the verb `이다` which means `to be`. As you can see there are two different versions, which one you use depends on the last character used in the block before them.

- `이에요` is used after a consonant.
- `예요` is used after a vowel.

## Using 이에요 and 예요

Since 이다 means something is something we can also use it to describe other things.

<Aside>
There are more casual ways to say 저는, e.g. 너는, but those could be rude depending on the situation. This will be covered in a later chapter. Being polite is better than being (accidentally) rude.
</Aside>
