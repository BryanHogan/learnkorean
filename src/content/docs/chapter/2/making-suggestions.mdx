How to make suggestions in Korean?

In Korean there are several ways to make suggestions, many depend on how polite you want to be.

## Saying `let's`

Saying `let's` is suggesting that someone else does something together with you.

For acquaintances and in non-formal situations you can use the adverb `같이` (together) with the present tense `요` form: `같이 + 요 Form`

<Sentence
    ko="**같이** 가요"
    en="Let's go (together)."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="**같이** 밥 먹어요"
    en="Let's eat (together)."
/>

## Honorific `let's`

When making a suggestion to someone else you are also talking about them, it's like saying `(We) do (verb) together`.

## Saying `shall we`

Another common way to make a suggestion is by asking `Shall we?` or `Shall I?`.

For situations where you want to be extra polite and would use honorific speech you can use the honorif ending `(으시다)` with `Shall we?`.

<Sentence
    ko="같이 식사하러 가실까요?"
    en="Shall we go eat a meal together?"
/>

`Verb Stem + (을 / ㄹ) 까요`

1. Get the verb stem.
2. Depending on the ending:
    - If it ends in a consonant: Attach `을까요`.
    - If it ends in a vowel: Attach `ㄹ까요`.
    - However, if it ends in a `ㄹ`: Attach `까요`.
    - However, if it ends in a `ㅂ`: Remove `ㅂ`, then attach `우`, then `ㄹ 까요`.

Example sentences:

<Sentence
    ko="어디 **갈까요**?"
    en="Where shall we go?"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="괴일을 **먹을까요**?"
    en="Shall we eat fruit?"
/>
<Sentence
    ko="뭘 **만들까요**?"
    en="What shall we make?"
/>
