Adverbs are used to add a description to a verb, a phrase or another adverb. Many descriptive verbs can be changed into adverbs.

## Building adverbs

To turn a descriptive verb into an adverb:

`Descriptive verb stem + 게`

<Sentence
    ko="**늦게** 와요."
    en="I am coming late."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="이안 씨는 **행복하게** 웃었어요."
    en="Ian smiled happily."
/>

`재미있다` can become `재미있게`. This means "in a manner that is entertaining or fun", or more naturally, "to enjoy".

<Sentence
    ko="어제 친구랑 **재미있게** 놀았어요."
    en="I enjoyed hanging out with my friend yesterday."
/>

`맛있다` can become `맛있게`. This means in a manner that tastes delicious, or more naturally, "to enjoy."

<Sentence
    ko="라면을 **맛있게** 먹었어요."
    en="I enjoyed the ramen."
/>

If the descriptive verb ends with a `하다` there are two ways it might turn into an adverb though.

## 하다 adverbs

Descriptive verbs that end with `하다` change into either `하게` or `히` when turning into an adverb. These can be irregular.

- `조용하다` becomes `조용히`. (quiet → quietly)
- `위험하다` becomes `위험하게`. (to be dangerous → dangerously)
- `안전하다` can become either `안전하게` or `안전히`. (to be safe → safely)
- `완전하다` can become either `완전히` or `완전하게`. (to be complete → completely)
- `강하다` becomes `강하게`. (to be strong → strongly)
- `약하다` becomes `약하게`. (to be weak → weakly)

*`조용히` and `안전하게` are generally more common though.*

## Unique adverbs

Many adverbs don't end with `게` or `히`, for example `더` (more), `빨리` (quickly) and `아주` (very).

More examples of such adverbs:

- 실수로: By accident or mistakenly

<Sentence
    ko="제 숙제를 **실수로** 버렸어요."
    en="I threw away my homework by accident."
/>

- 일부러: On purpose or intentionally

<Sentence
    ko="저는 **일부러** 빨리 했어요."
    en="I did it quickly on purpose."
/>

- 잠시 and 잠깐: A brief moment (`잠깐` is more common in informal speech)

<Sentence
    ko="**잠시만** 기다려 주세요."
    en="Please wait a moment."
/>
<Sentence
    ko="**잠깐** 기다려."
    en="Wait a second."
/>

- 혼자(서): Alone (without others)

<Sentence
    ko="저는 서울에서 **혼자서** 살고 있어요."
    en="I am living alone in Seoul."
/>

- 천천히: Slowly

<Sentence
    ko="**천천히** 말해 주세요."
    en="Please speak slowly."
/>

Unlike `있다` (to exist) which conjugates regularly to `있게`, `없다` (to not exist) conjugates to `없이` (without). `없이` is used directly after a noun, without the subject marker.

<Sentence
    ko="우산 **없이** 밖에 나갔어요."
    en="I went outside without an umbrella."
/>
