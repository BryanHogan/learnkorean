How do you say that someone or something is something in Korean?

## Introducing your name with 이다

`이에요` and `예요` both come from the verb `이다`, which means `to be`. The version you use depends on the final letter of the word before it:

- `이에요` is used after a consonant.
- `예요` is used after a vowel.

Examples:

<Sentence
  ko="저는 이안**이에요**."
  en="I am Ian."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="저는 사라**예요**."
  en="I am Sarah."
/>

`저는` comes from `저` (meaning I or me) and the topic marker 는.

:::note
There are more casual ways to say 저는, e.g. 나는, but those could be rude depending on the situation. See the chapter on [casual speech](/chapter/casual-speech). Being polite is better than being (accidentally) rude.
:::

## Using 이에요 and 예요

Since 이다 means "to be," we can also use it to describe other things.

Examples:

<Sentence
  ko="이거 바나나**예요**."
  en="This is a banana."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="이거 커피**예요**."
  en="This is coffee."
/>

`이거` means "this thing". There are other ways to say "this", depending on the context.
