How to say something is something in Korean?

## Introducing your name with 이다

To say:

<Sentence
  ko="저는 이안이에요."
  en="I am Ian."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="저는 사라예요."
  en="I am Sarah."
/>

`저는` comes from `저` (meaning I or me) and 는 which will be covered later in this unit.

<Aside>
There are more casual ways to say 저는, e.g. 나는, but those could be rude depending on the situation. This will be covered in a later chapter. Being polite is better than being (accidentally) rude.
</Aside>


`이에요` and `예요` both come from the verb `이다` which means `to be`. As you can see there are two different versions, which one you use depends on the last character used in the block before them.

- `이에요` is used after a consonant.
- `예요` is used after a vowel.

## Using 이에요 and 예요

Since 이다 means something is something we can also use it to describe other things.

Examples:

<Sentence
  ko="이거 바나나예요."
  en="This is a banana."
/>

<Sentence
  ko="이거 커피예요."
  en="This is coffee."
/>

`이거` means "this thing". There is some nuance here and other ways to say "this", but that will be covered in a later chapter.
