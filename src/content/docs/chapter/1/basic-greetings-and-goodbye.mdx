---
title: "Saying hello and goodbye"
description: "Learn how to say hello and goodbye in Korean for different situations."
---

Korean greetings depend on who you are speaking to and, when saying goodbye, who is leaving.

## Saying hello

How you say "hello" depends on who you are talking to. Korean has different politeness levels.

To close friends who are your age or younger:

<Sentence
  ko="안녕!"
  en="Hello!"
/>

To anyone else:

<Sentence
  ko="안녕하세요."
  en="Hello."
/>

## Saying goodbye

To close friends who are your age or younger:

<Sentence
  ko="안녕!"
  en="Goodbye!"
/>

Yes, "goodbye" is the same word as saying "hello".

But to other people, when you need to be more formal, you have two options:

You are leaving, and they are staying:

<Sentence
  ko="안녕히 **계세요**."
  en="Goodbye. (Literally: 'Stay in peace.')"
/>

You are staying, and they are leaving:

<Sentence
  ko="안녕히 **가세요**."
  en="Goodbye. (Literally: 'Go in peace.')"
/>
